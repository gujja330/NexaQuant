I. Executive Summary

An investment platform that learns from every decision it makes.

AEGIS is a research platform built from fifteen composable engines. Each engine reads from validated upstream outputs, produces auditable artifacts, and never mutates state. The result is not a stock screener — it is an evidence layer suitable for institutional use, in which every recommendation traces a full explanatory path through market intelligence, portfolio construction, learning, calibration, and a live knowledge graph.

The platform is advisory-only by construction, per ARCH001A Article V clause 5.1. Recommendations are never auto-executed. Every module is deterministic — identical inputs produce identical outputs — and every output is written to disk before the next module reads it. This makes the entire investment lifecycle inspectable, replayable, and defensible.

The system compounds intelligence. The knowledge graph consumes twelve upstream engines; confidence calibration consumes trade history; the champion-vs-challenger framework consumes strategy backtests. Adding a new engine adds new edges to the graph, new inputs to the learning loop, and new evidence to every recommendation.

VI. Confidence Calibration · Evidence

Raw confidence had no discriminative signal. Three engines detected it independently.

DEV025 (learning), DEV027 (doctor), and DEV029 (calibration) each surfaced the same finding from a different angle. Every trade wins ~58% regardless of stated confidence. Platt scaling correctly collapses raw confidence to the base rate — an honest, evidence-driven answer.

DEV029 · Held-out test, 1,060 trades

Raw ECE 0.287 on average 29pp miscalibrated
Calibrated ECE 0.002 Platt scaling · -140×

Raw · 0.80–0.90920 0.850.589 +26pp

Under ARCH001A Article V clause 5.1 (advisory-only), the calibrated confidence is surfaced in the outputs but does not automatically rewrite downstream recommendations. The finding is a strong signal that raw confidence is a rank-ordering label, not a probability — the correct response is to redesign the confidence feature-set, not to patch the label.

IX. Institutional Features

What separates AEGIS from a screener.

A screener ranks stocks against static rules. AEGIS treats every recommendation as an evidence chain that can be inspected, challenged, and — where wrong — diagnosed.

Capability Traditional Screener AEGIS
Learning Static rules; no feedback loop 1,060 trades analysed across 6 outcome dimensions (DEV025)
Confidence calibration Raw score, uncalibrated Five methods competed; Platt selected; ECE 0.287 → 0.002 (DEV029)
Failure diagnosis None 15 diagnostic rules; 677 diagnoses fired; overconfidence is #1 (DEV027)
Strategy governance Single hard-coded ranking Champion vs Challenger with 4-gate promotion recommender (DEV030)
Knowledge graph Flat list 581 nodes across 10 entity types; 57 communities discovered (DEV031)
Explainability "Because the score was high" Full path: Company → Industry → Sector → Regime + Champion + Signals + Outcome (DEV031-B)
