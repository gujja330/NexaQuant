Honest Success Factors
What Actually Works:
1. Consistency Over Automation: Regular, quality posting beats sporadic automation
2. Value-First Approach: Always provide value before promoting
3. Authentic Engagement: Real relationships drive real revenue
4. Data-Driven Decisions: Track everything and optimize based on real data
5. Platform Mastery: Master 2-3 platforms rather than spreading thin
What Causes Failure:
1. Over-Automation: Platforms detect and penalize automated behavior
2. Poor Content Quality: Free AI without human oversight produces poor content
3. Inconsistent Posting: Irregular activity reduces visibility and engagement
4. Too Much Promotion: Excessive promotion without value destroys trust
5. Unrealistic Expectations: Expecting fast results leads to poor decisions
